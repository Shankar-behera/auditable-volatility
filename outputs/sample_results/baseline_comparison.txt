BASELINE COMPARISON
==================================================
Windows: 170 (size=64, step=10)
Correlation: CETE energy      vs realized volatility = 0.9560
Correlation: GARCH(1,1) cond.  vs realized volatility = 0.7295
Correlation: CETE energy      vs GARCH(1,1)           = 0.7271

Interpretation:
CETE's correlation with realized volatility is very high because both are
computed FROM THE SAME WINDOW (non-causal): by Parseval's theorem, total FFT
spectral power and time-domain variance of a window are proportional. CETE's
own pre-dynamics spectral power correlates >0.999 with plain np.var(window)
on this data -- the entropy-reweighting and relaxation dynamics are a
near-constant rescaling of a one-line variance calculation, not new
information. GARCH(1,1)'s conditional volatility, by contrast, is a genuine
causal forecast built only from PAST returns, so its correlation with the
same (non-causal) realized-vol window is naturally lower -- it is solving a
harder problem, not a worse one.
